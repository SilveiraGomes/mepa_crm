# P0.10 — Finance + Payroll — Final Independent Audit

Audit date: 2026-10-03

Audit branch: `p010-finance-payroll`

Audited baseline: `41a0927186976fe487fdf58fd3f5587ca6594986`

Merge base with `main`: `a3d7c5f3405c5c17ee5783251f1cf32068a7cc13`

## 1. Scope

Independent L7 audit of P0.10 Finance and Payroll against the approved ADRs, contracts, schema, security boundaries, evidence packs and production-disabled release posture. The audit reproduced schema creation, domain tests, mutation validators, regressions, build, PWA output and browser journeys on a fresh MySQL audit database.

## 2. Baseline and provenance

The audit started from a clean worktree at the expected baseline. `main` is an ancestor of the audited branch; the branch is 27 commits ahead. Commit history separates Finance schema/foundation, custody and transfers, accrual/bank/budget/closing, reporting, Payroll schema/foundation, engine, API/UI, fixes and evidence. No suspicious history rewrite was found.

## 3. Schema parity

Fresh Wave 5 construction applied 203 migrations and produced 189 tables. P0.10, P0.9, P0.8, P0.7 and P0.6 parity checks passed. P0.9 was stable in three consecutive runs, with 55 indexes compared and no retry.

## 4. Finance reproduction

Finance F1A–F1D contracts and regression suites passed. The reproduction covered balanced journal posting, immutable posted entries, receivables/payables and settlement, bank matching, budget approval, period closing, internal transfers with custody, consolidated reporting and explicit own-versus-consolidated perimeter handling.

## 5. Payroll reproduction

Payroll F2A and F2B contracts passed. The lifecycle `DRAFT -> CALCULATED -> APPROVED -> POSTED -> PAID` was reproduced with separation of duties, stale-input rejection, immutable snapshots, idempotent POST/PAY behavior, Finance aggregation and production default disabled.

## 6. Accounting cross-check

An independent arithmetic fixture produced gross `257654.00`, employee deductions `13129.68`, employer charges `19012.32` and net pay `244524.32`. POST generated balanced debit and credit totals of `276666.32`. PAY cleared the net-pay liability by `244524.32`; statutory liabilities of `32142.00` remained open. POST increased DRE expense by `276666.32` without cash movement; PAY left DRE unchanged and applied `244524.32` in DOAF. Finance consolidation independently reproduced the `100/60/40` revenue chain without double counting internal movement.

## 7. Concurrency

Deterministic MySQL barriers, rather than timing sleeps, covered close-versus-post, concurrent receipt, concurrent settlement, budget approval, input-change-versus-approval, duplicate POST, duplicate PAY and reporting-snapshot-versus-posting. All required C-A1–C-A8 outcomes passed with a single authoritative result and no duplicate accounting effects.

## 8. Security and privacy

Territorial Finance and HR authorization, concealment of inaccessible records, rejection of internal identifiers at public boundaries, salary exclusion from People and Finance responses, HR-only salary visibility, value-free audit events and cumulative Files authority all passed. Payroll posts only aggregated accounting amounts into Finance; individual compensation remains protected.

## 9. Mutation verification

Payroll F2B detected 20/20 contract mutations and F2A detected 16/16; all Finance mutation validators passed and restored their sources byte-for-byte. The independently selected high-risk set was detected: broken double entry, internal-transfer misclassification, historical-perimeter leakage, stale payroll approval, duplicate POST/PAY and salary-privacy leakage.

## 10. Regressions

Finance passed F1A `26/664`, F1B `21/1225`, F1C `46/1477`, F1D `4/40` and F1D evidence `32/775` tests/assertions. Payroll passed F2B `47/1720` and F2A `29/825`. Other passing suites were Territorial `13/151`, Files `27/1550`, People context `8/220` and People person `15/1354`.

## 11. UI and build

Web tests passed `530`; HTTP contracts passed `828`; authorization passed `20`; application validation passed `1513`; database documentation validated 208 tables with no errors; Wave 5 schema drift was zero. Vitest, lint and production build passed, including generated `sw.js`. Playwright passed the production-disabled phase `12/12` and production-enabled F2B phase `4/4`. Desktop and mobile screenshots were manually inspected: consolidated views are explicit, Payroll status/actions are usable, and paid-run Finance aggregation and remaining statutory liabilities are visible without exposing individual salary data.

## 12. Test infrastructure

Three audit-harness defects were found and corrected: the PWA build used an unwritable system temp path; Windows process shutdown could leave the disabled API bound during the enabled phase; and Playwright non-zero exit codes were not promoted to runner failures. The corrections are confined to the audit runner. The final run verified service startup and shutdown, isolated temporary data, removal of fixtures/keyrings/results, no evidence or log secret leaks and production default `false`.

## 13. Findings

No PRODUCT or EVIDENCE findings remain. The three TEST_INFRA findings are resolved and were reverified by a successful focused end-to-end run.

## 14. Known non-blocking debt

Deferred scope remains: proration; 13th-month, holiday and adjustment processing; negative-net handling; richer IRT bases; multi-account payment; close-period warning UX; stronger accessible names for dialogs; friendlier accounting labels; and the operational activation checklist. These items are non-blocking because unsupported cases fail closed and Payroll remains disabled by default in production.

## 15. Verdict

**APPROVED — L7.** P0.10 Finance + Payroll satisfies the audited schema, accounting, concurrency, security/privacy, mutation, regression, UI and release-gating requirements. The branch is recommended for merge to `main`; this audit does not merge or push it.
