## Delivered

- `ReportPerimeterResolver` resolves the subtree from `unit_parent_periods` at the report end date. It does not use the current `parent_id` as the historical source.
- `FinanceReportingService` derives every figure from `POSTED` journal lines/subledgers in one `REPEATABLE READ` transaction. No mutable balance, dashboard cache, materialized report or new table was introduced.
- Own DRE is accrual based. Internal transfers do not touch Revenue/Expense; capitalized investments remain outside immediate DRE expense.
- Own DOAF exposes opening balance, external receipts, internal receipts and origins, external applications, internal remittances and destinations, transit and closing custody.
- Consolidated DRE sums the economic perimeter without converting transfers into revenue/expense. Consolidated DOAF eliminates completed internal custody movements, retains boundary `INTO_PERIMETER` / `OUT_OF_PERIMETER` movements and treats internal transit as custody.
- Treasury, interunit control, transfer reconciliation, financial movements, current approved budget versus actual, receivables and payables are bounded read models.
- The API exposes dashboard, the 16 V1 reports and audited CSV export. Responses use public identifiers and pass `FinanceOutput::assertSafe`.
- Authority is `FINANCE_REPORT` on the unit; consolidated reports additionally require `FINANCE_CONSOLIDATED_VIEW` on the root with current subtree coverage. Drill-down projects only units with `FINANCE_VIEW`. Unknown and wrong-scope roots are concealed.
- The UI now contains Visão Geral, Contribuições and Relatórios, explicit PRÓPRIO/CONSOLIDADO selection, month/quarter/semester/year filters, official print header, authorized drill-down and authenticated CSV download. In-kind contributions without an approved ledger entry explicitly show no monetary effect.
