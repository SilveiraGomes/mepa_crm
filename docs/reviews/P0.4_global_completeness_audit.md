## P0.10 closure

P0.10 is **COMPLETE / L7**. Its approved scope closes:

- Finance, including own-unit and consolidated-subtree reporting without duplicated internal revenue;
- HR/Compensation only for the P0.10 employment and remuneration foundation;
- Payroll calculation and lifecycle through aggregated Finance POST and PAY;
- Finance/Payroll privacy, segregation, audit, concurrency and production-disabled activation posture.

This classification does **not** declare the entire HR domain complete. Capabilities beyond the approved P0.10 employment/compensation scope remain represented by the separate `HR — global scope` row.

## Global invariants retained

- Person remains the single identity root; no completed vertical creates parallel people.
- Membership and participation remain relations to Person.
- Organizational scope follows the recursive national tree.
- Internal financial transfers do not duplicate revenue.
- Payroll posts aggregated accounting amounts; Finance receives no individual salary detail.
- Historical assignments, transfers, compensation and accounting events are preserved.
- Production Payroll remains disabled by default and contains no seeded legal rates.

## Open system-level blockers

P0.10 has no open PRODUCT or EVIDENCE blockers. Global project blockers remain outside that vertical, including production infrastructure/D-08, approved MFA for critical profiles, and incomplete verticals shown below L7 in the matrix.
